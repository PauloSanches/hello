02 / PEOPLE & TRUST

The people behind
the architecture.

Helping an international team find stability, shared ownership and a way forward.

Read the story + about people and trust

The starting point

When I joined a public-sector modernisation programme, the international team was dealing with instability and recurring departures.

What I worked on

I brought coaching, shared objectives and a product mindset into the team’s day-to-day work. Alongside that, an Architecture Board and documented decisions gave people a clearer way to agree on the technical direction.

What changed

The team became more stable, and trust with the client grew. People and architecture moved forward together.
